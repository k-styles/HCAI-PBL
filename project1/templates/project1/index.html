What this app does

The pipeline from lecture 1, with the decisions left visible.

Choose a {% term "model" %}, {% term "test-set" "split the rows" %}, train it once for every value of its {% term "hyperparameter" %}, and {% term "accuracy" "score the results" %}. The interesting question is not how to run that loop — it is who gets to make each of those four choices.

Here the split, the {% term "cross-validation" "fold count" %}, the values to try, the score and the {% term "one-se-rule" "selection rule" %} are all yours. The mechanical part — building balanced folds, running the sweep, keeping the {% term "test-set" "test set" %} out of the selection — is automatic. There is also {% term "automl" "a button that makes every one of those choices for you" %}, so you can see what gets decided in your name.
